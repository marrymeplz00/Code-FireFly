## About the Project

**FireFly** is a 2D Pixel Art Narrative Adventure game developed using **Unity**.

The game takes players on an emotional journey through a mysterious forest where light represents hope, courage, and encouragement.

Players explore the forest, solve environmental puzzles, and use the power of fireflies to restore ancient altars and remove barriers blocking their path.

The project focuses on **atmospheric exploration, narrative-driven gameplay, and emotional storytelling.**

## Core Gameplay Features

### Atmospheric Exploration
Explore a mysterious forest filled with hidden paths, environmental details, and secrets waiting to be discovered.

### Narrative Puzzle Solving
Interact with the environment and solve puzzles to progress through the story.

### Firefly Mechanics
Use the power of fireflies as a key gameplay element to navigate darkness and interact with the world.

### Altar & Barrier Restoration
Restore ancient altars and overcome obstacles to unlock new paths throughout the forest.

### Emotional Storytelling
Experience a narrative centered around fear, hope, and the courage to move forward.
